Janus AI — The Bloomberg of Real Estate
The Problem
Real estate is the largest asset class in the world, yet the investment process is deeply fragmented. An investor or developer today has to juggle:
Sourcing tools (PropStream, CoStar, Reonomy, MLS access).

Underwriting models (Excel spreadsheets, manual comps).

Legal reviews (lawyers manually pulling deeds, zoning, liens).

Financing outreach (mortgage brokers, banks, lenders).

Agent/broker communication (calls, email threads).

Investment committee prep (assembling decks, stress testing manually).

The system hasn’t changed in decades. Investors still operate as if every deal were a one-off.

The Solution — Janus AI
Janus AI is the first end-to-end platform that consolidates the full investment lifecycle into a single hub.
Through a system of specialized AI agents, Janus transforms the investment process:
Deal Sourcing & Discovery

Scan millions of properties for distressed, undervalued, or high-potential assets.

Surface leads in real time with explainable insights.
 
Document Ingestion & Centralization

Upload deeds, leases, inspections, or financials.

Janus parses and standardizes all documents into structured data.

Automated Underwriting & Analysis

Instant cash-flow models, rent comps, renovation scenarios, and cap rates.

Sensitivity analyses and stress tests run automatically.

Legal & Compliance Checks

Automated review of ownership, zoning, permits, liens, and tax history.

AI legal assistant generates reports for lawyers to validate.

AI Investment Committee

A panel of agents debates pros and cons, surfacing risks and opportunities.

Produces an in-depth, human-readable investment memo with recommendations.

Execution & Closing

Contact property owners directly.

Generate offer letters, contracts, and financing packages.

Connect to lenders and partners — all from within Janus.

Post-Acquisition Intelligence

Track renovations, tenant demand, and refinancing opportunities.

Ongoing monitoring to maximize asset value.
